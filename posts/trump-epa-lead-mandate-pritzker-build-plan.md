# Lead

## Trump EPA Upholds Lead Pipe Replacement Rule

In a notable departure from its deregulatory posture, the Trump administration [announced Friday](https://www.chicagotribune.com/2026/02/20/trump-administration-biden-era-mandates-lead-pipes/) that it will uphold the Biden-era rule requiring most cities to replace their lead service lines within ten years.

The Lead and Copper Rule Improvements, finalized in 2024, also tightened how lead levels are measured — a change that could flag more communities as out of compliance. For Chicago, which has [more lead service lines](https://grist.org/accountability/chicago-lead-pipe-service-line-replacement-plan-epa/) than any other American city (~412,000), the mandate's survival means the federal clock keeps ticking even as the city [struggles to spend](https://chicago.suntimes.com/environment/2025/09/22/lead-pipes-chicago-city-council-notification-federal-loans-unspent) its $325 million federal loan before the 2027 deadline.

The EPA's stance on replacement stands in tension with the same administration's [attempt to cut](https://chicago.suntimes.com/health/2026/02/12/federal-judge-temporarily-blocks-trump-cuts-to-hiv-lead-poisoning-prevention-funds) CDC lead poisoning *prevention* grants to Illinois and three other states — cuts that remain [temporarily blocked](https://www.cidrap.umn.edu/public-health/cdc-funding-cuts-4-states-limbo-after-judge-s-ruling) by a federal judge. In short: the feds are telling cities to replace their pipes faster while simultaneously pulling funding for the public health workers who track lead's toll.

# Housing & Abundance

## Pritzker Unveils Statewide Zoning Overhaul

Governor Pritzker used his budget address this week to [propose the BUILD plan](https://capitolnewsillinois.com/news/pritzker-to-propose-statewide-zoning-laws-to-spur-homebuilding-limit-local-control/) (Building Up Illinois Developments) — a sweeping statewide housing package that would eliminate single-family-only zoning on lots over 2,500 square feet, legalize duplexes through four-flats and ADUs on all residential land, prohibit parking minimums for middle housing, and establish statewide permitting timelines with third-party inspector fallbacks.

The plan includes [$250 million in funding](https://chicagoagentmagazine.com/2026/02/18/pritzker-comprehensive-housing-plan/): $100M for site preparation infrastructure, $100M for middle housing development through IHDA, and $50M for homebuyer assistance. Illinois is roughly [142,000 units short](https://therealdeal.com/chicago/2026/02/18/pritzker-proposes-statewide-zoning-overhaul-for-housing/) of housing demand and needs to nearly double its current pace of homebuilding.

The Illinois Municipal League [pushed back Friday](https://www.cities929.com/2026/02/20/municipal-league-housing-reform-could-strip-authority-from-local-communities/), warning the plan "removes the local land use zoning authority" from cities. [Housing Action Illinois](https://housingactionil.org/blog/2026/02/19/statement-on-building-up-illinois-developments-from-housing-organizations/) endorsed it. This will be one to watch in Springfield.

## Foundry Park: Lincoln Yards Reborn

Almost seven years after the original Lincoln Yards megadevelopment was approved, City Council on Wednesday [greenlit Foundry Park](https://blockclubchicago.org/2026/02/18/foundry-park-development-at-former-lincoln-yards-site-approved-by-city-council/), a scaled-back but still substantial mixed-use development along the North Branch of the Chicago River.

The first phase brings 709 housing units (95 affordable) across four buildings, anchored by a 39-story tower at Kingsbury and Cortland with office, residential, and retail space. Southport Avenue will be extended south to the river as the project's commercial spine, and the plan includes a new riverwalk and possible extension of the 606/Bloomingdale Trail.

Ald. Brian Hopkins (2nd) [cast the lone "no" vote](https://www.thedailyline.com/chicago-city-council-zoning-committee-february-2026-meeting-recap-foundry-park-large-housing-developments-downtown-exceed-parking-limits-ordinance), arguing the area will lose $800 million in public infrastructure that was mandated under the original Lincoln Yards plan. Construction could begin as early as fall 2026.

## Missing Middle: Round 3 Announced

DPD on February 5 [selected 35 new buildings](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/dcd/provdrs/ec_dev/news/2026/february/new-missing-middle-residential-projects-selected-garfield-park-mckinley-park.html) containing 99 housing units for East Garfield Park, West Garfield Park, and McKinley Park. The $35.5 million in projects will replace 24 vacant city-owned lots with two-, three-, and four-flats sold to owner-occupants earning up to 140% of area median income ($134,400 for a two-person household).

This brings the Missing Middle pipeline to over 300 buildings across six South and West Side neighborhoods. [Ground broke in North Lawndale](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/mayor/press_room/press_releases/2026/january/missing-middle-groundbreaking.html) in January on the program's first seven two-flats.

## ADU Countdown: 39 Days

City Council on Wednesday [expanded ADU eligibility](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/mayor/press_room/press_releases/2026/february/city-council-february.html) to four additional wards — meaning more aldermen are opting their single-family zones into the program ahead of the **April 1** effective date. The [citywide ADU ordinance](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/doh/provdrs/homeowners/svcs/adu-ordinance.html) will more than double the area where ADUs may be built. Questions: [adu@cityofchicago.org](mailto:adu@cityofchicago.org).

# Bikes

## Archer Avenue: The Race Is On

The Archer Avenue bike lane controversy has officially entered electoral politics. Brighton Park business owner Claudia Zuno [announced her candidacy](https://blockclubchicago.org/2026/02/18/fight-over-bike-lanes-in-brighton-park-propels-claudia-zuno-to-run-for-12th-ward-alderperson/) for 12th Ward alderperson on February 16, running against incumbent Ald. Julia Ramirez with a pledge to "remove these concrete barriers."

Zuno, a second-generation photographer and daughter of immigrant business owners, emerged from the [Archer Guardians](https://southsideweekly.com/archer-ave-bike-lanes-tension-fuels-political-ambitions/) protest group that has rallied weekly since December. [Streetsblog Chicago](https://chi.streetsblog.org/2026/02/17/archer-nimby-claudia-zuno-an-ally-of-trump-apologists-urban-center-and-ald-lopez-is-running-for-alder-with-a-plan-to-remove-obstructive-bike-lanes) reported ties between the campaign and The Urban Center, a charter school advocacy group that has coordinated opposition efforts; Zuno says she is "not aligned or affiliated with any PAC or organizations."

For context: Archer and Kedzie saw [6,439 crashes, 1,426 injuries, and 14 deaths](https://blockclubchicago.org/2026/02/18/fight-over-bike-lanes-in-brighton-park-propels-claudia-zuno-to-run-for-12th-ward-alderperson/) from 2019 to 2023. Half of those killed were pedestrians.

# Food

## Bloom: Last Call

Today is the final day of service at [Bloom Plant Based Kitchen](https://bloompb.com/), the Wicker Park vegan restaurant that earned a spot among the city's best during its four-year run. Chef Rodolfo Cuadros [told the Tribune](https://www.chicagotribune.com/2026/01/05/chicago-vegan-restaurant-closures/) that rising costs and the misperception that plant-based eating is a fad made the venture unsustainable — but [plans to return](https://vegnews.com/chicago-vegan-restaurants-closing) with a new concept that "carries Bloom's spirit forward."

Bloom joins Kitchen 17, Native Foods, Chicago Raw, and XMarket in a wave of vegan closures that PETA's [2024 designation](https://vegconomist.com/gastronomy-food-service/food-service/chicago-vegan-restaurant-bloom-close-earning-spot-among-citys-best/) of Chicago as the country's most vegan-friendly city couldn't forestall. Still standing: [Kale My Name](https://vegnews.com/chicago-vegan-restaurants-closing), The Chicago Diner, The Black Vegan, and [Penelope's Vegan Taqueria](https://vegnews.com/chicago-first-vegan-taqueria), which is expanding to Andersonville.

Meanwhile, in Evanston, a new fully plant-based restaurant called [17](https://dailynorthwestern.com/2026/01/27/city/from-surfboard-to-stovetop-vegan-restaurant-17-comes-to-downtown/) opened last month at the Hilton Orrington — seating 17 people, serving 17 dishes, and run by a family of four.

## Black Restaurant Week: Final Weekend

[Chicago Black Restaurant Week](https://chiblackrestaurantweek.com/) wraps up **tomorrow, Sunday February 22**, with [43 participating businesses](https://chicago.suntimes.com/restaurants/2026/02/02/chicago-black-restaurant-week-restaurant-list).
