# Council & Governance

## Today: Two Closely Watched Votes

City Council convenes at 10 a.m. today with two measures that advanced from committee earlier this month.

**COPA Sanctuary Enforcement**: The [amendment giving COPA explicit authority](https://news.wttw.com/2026/02/02/alderpeople-say-copa-should-investigate-chicago-police-conduct-during-immigration-raids) to investigate alleged Welcoming City violations appears on track to pass. COPA Chief Administrator LaKenya White says her office is prepared to investigate [40 complaints](https://news.wttw.com/2026/01/27/key-city-panel-advances-plan-give-copa-power-probe-chicago-police-conduct-during) filed since June involving CPD's interactions with federal immigration agents. The measure advanced from committee 19-3.

"It is not enough to be a welcoming city on paper," Mayor Johnson said before the vote.

**Parking Scofflaw Pilot**: Ald. Daniel La Spata's [citizen enforcement measure](https://chicago.suntimes.com/city-hall/2026/02/09/parking-scofflaws-ordinance-daniel-laspata-council-traffic-safety-committee-approval) would create a Street Operations Task Force targeting bus lane, bike lane, and crosswalk violations. Under the [revised ordinance](https://blockclubchicago.org/2026/02/10/chicagoans-could-report-parking-scofflaws-via-311-and-see-better-enforcement-under-pilot/), only commercial vehicles would be eligible for citizen reports via 311. The dispatch system must be operational by December 31.

# Lead

## Federal Judge Blocks Prevention Funding Cuts

A [federal judge temporarily blocked](https://chicago.suntimes.com/health/2026/02/12/federal-judge-temporarily-blocks-trump-cuts-to-hiv-lead-poisoning-prevention-funds) the Trump administration's cuts to CDC grants for lead poisoning prevention, ensuring funding continues flowing to Illinois while the case plays out.

Illinois Attorney General Kwame Raoul [sued last week](https://chicago.suntimes.com/health/2026/02/11/federal-cuts-hiv-lead-poisoning-prevention-grants-raoul) over approximately $600 million in public health cuts that would have ended lead prevention grants to 25 local health departments statewide. "Trump is playing politics with critical public health funding," Raoul said.

The cuts targeted California, Colorado, Illinois, and Minnesota—all states that have clashed with the administration on immigration policy.

# Bikes

## Northwest Side: New Greenways Proposed

CDOT is proposing a [network of bike lanes and greenways](https://nadignewspapers.com/new-bike-lanes-greenways-proposed-for-jefferson-park-portage-park-on-chicagos-nw-side/) for Jefferson Park and Portage Park in the 30th, 38th, and 45th wards.

Proposed greenways would run on Sunnyside, Berteau, Melvina, Menard, and Long avenues. New bike lanes are planned for Central, Laramie, and Montrose.

**Public meeting: March 5, 6-7 p.m.** at the Portage Park Senior Center (4150 N. Long Ave.). More information at [chicago.gov/portagepark](https://www.chicago.gov/portagepark).

## Grand Avenue: Coalition Still Pushing for Buses Over Bikes

The [coalition of business owners](https://www.cbsnews.com/chicago/news/coalition-opposes-bike-lane-construction-grand-avenue-west-town-downtown/) opposing protected bike lanes on Grand Avenue Phase 2 (Damen to Ogden) continues to advocate for dedicated bus lanes instead. Phase 2 is expected to begin later this year.

# Food

## Bloom: Three Days Left

[Bloom Plant Based Kitchen](https://bloompb.com/) enters its final days in Wicker Park. The last day of service is **Saturday, February 21**. Chef Rodolfo Cuadros [says the team plans to return](https://vegnews.com/chicago-vegan-restaurants-closing) with a new vegan concept.

## Black Restaurant Week: Final Stretch

[Chicago Black Restaurant Week](https://chiblackrestaurantweek.com/) runs through **Sunday, February 22**, with [nearly 40 participating businesses](https://chicago.suntimes.com/restaurants/2026/02/02/chicago-black-restaurant-week-restaurant-list) offering fixed-price specials. The [11th-annual event](https://thetriibe.com/2026/02/chicago-black-restaurant-week-enters-11th-year-of-boosting-the-citys-foodie-scene/) honors Carter G. Woodson's founding of Negro History Week in Chicago exactly 100 years ago.

First-time participants include Strugglebeard Bakery and Mahari.

# ADU Countdown: 42 Days

Chicago's [citywide ADU ordinance](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/doh/provdrs/homeowners/svcs/adu-ordinance.html) takes effect **April 1, 2026**. The ordinance passed unanimously (46-0) in September 2025, [more than doubling](https://www.chicagocityscape.com/blog/what-to-know-about-chicago-s-permanent-adu-ordinance-9e9052e63a) the area where ADUs may be built.
