# Council Recap

## COPA Sanctuary Enforcement: Blocked

Four aldermen [moved to defer](https://www.fox32chicago.com/news/city-council-hemp-ban-veto-copa) a proposal that would have given COPA authority to investigate alleged violations of the city's Welcoming City Ordinance. Alds. Anthony Beale (9th), Raymond Lopez (15th), Silvana Tabares (23rd), and Anthony Napolitano (41st) blocked the measure, which had [advanced from committee 19-3](https://news.wttw.com/2026/02/02/alderpeople-say-copa-should-investigate-chicago-police-conduct-during-immigration-raids) earlier this month.

The ordinance could return for a vote next month.

## Scofflaw Pilot: Defeated 16-31

Ald. Daniel La Spata's [citizen enforcement measure](https://blockclubchicago.org/2026/02/10/chicagoans-could-report-parking-scofflaws-via-311-and-see-better-enforcement-under-pilot/) for bike lane, bus lane, and crosswalk parking violations was voted down by the full council. The proposal, which had [cleared committee](https://chicago.suntimes.com/city-hall/2026/02/09/parking-scofflaws-ordinance-daniel-laspata-council-traffic-safety-committee-approval) nine days earlier, would have created a Street Operations Task Force and dispatch system for 311 complaints.

Opposition centered on concerns from business owners about delivery vehicles that double-park in travel lanes.

## Hemp Veto Override: Failed

The council [failed to override](https://www.fox32chicago.com/news/city-council-hemp-ban-veto-copa) Mayor Johnson's veto of a ban on certain hemp product sales. Johnson had argued the ban would "force small businesses to close, eliminate jobs, and disproportionately affect shops owned by residents of color."

# Lead

## Aurora Adds Orthophosphate to Drinking Water

Aurora will begin adding [dipotassium orthophosphate](https://www.chicagotribune.com/2026/02/18/aurora-to-put-new-chemical-in-drinking-water-to-reduce-lead-levels-in-certain-homes/) to its water supply to reduce lead leaching from service lines. The chemical reacts with lead pipes to form a protective coating that seals in the lead.

Chicago and Elgin have also begun using orthophosphate. The decision follows Aurora's recent testing, which found lead levels above the action threshold in more than 10% of sampled homes. Since 2018, Aurora has replaced 2,988 lead service lines.

The federal judge's [temporary block](https://chicago.suntimes.com/health/2026/02/12/federal-judge-temporarily-blocks-trump-cuts-to-hiv-lead-poisoning-prevention-funds) on Trump administration cuts to CDC lead prevention grants remains in effect.

# Bikes

## Archer Avenue: Controversy Continues

The [protected bike lanes on Archer Avenue](https://southsideweekly.com/archer-ave-bike-lanes-tension-fuels-political-ambitions/) in Brighton Park have become entangled with electoral politics. While construction continues, opposition efforts have been coordinated partly through The Urban Center, a charter school advocacy group.

Tribune [letters to the editor](https://www.chicagotribune.com/2026/02/16/letters-021626-archer-avenue-bike-lanes/) published over the weekend show a range of community opinion—from "enthusiastic support" to outright opposition. Supporters note Brighton Park has 90% more traffic crashes than the city average, with 6,000 crashes and 13 fatalities in recent years.

## Northwest Side: Public Meeting March 5

CDOT's [proposed network](https://nadignewspapers.com/new-bike-lanes-greenways-proposed-for-jefferson-park-portage-park-on-chicagos-nw-side/) of bike lanes and greenways for Jefferson Park and Portage Park comes before residents at the Portage Park Senior Center (4150 N. Long Ave.) on March 5, 6-7 p.m.

# Food

## Bloom: Two Days Remain

[Bloom Plant Based Kitchen](https://bloompb.com/) closes Saturday, February 21. Chef Rodolfo Cuadros [plans to return](https://vegnews.com/chicago-vegan-restaurants-closing) with a new vegan concept.

## Black Restaurant Week: Final Stretch

[Chicago Black Restaurant Week](https://chiblackrestaurantweek.com/) continues through Sunday, February 22, with [nearly 40 participating businesses](https://blockclubchicago.org/2026/02/05/chicago-black-restaurant-week-is-back-with-some-new-additions/). First-time participants include [Mahari](https://chicago.suntimes.com/restaurants/2026/02/02/chicago-black-restaurant-week-restaurant-list), an African fusion restaurant in Hyde Park, and Strugglebeard Bakery, known for its vegan cookies.

The [11th-annual event](https://thetriibe.com/2026/02/chicago-black-restaurant-week-enters-11th-year-of-boosting-the-citys-foodie-scene/) marks 100 years since Carter G. Woodson founded Negro History Week in Chicago.
